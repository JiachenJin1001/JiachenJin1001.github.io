Jiachen Jin

Predoctoral Researcher
Department of Finance, NYU Stern School of Business

Hi, welcome! I am a predoc at NYU Stern, where I work with Peter Koudijs and Amanda Dos Santos. I graduated from the University of Chicago with an M.A. in Social Sciences, and before that from Shanghai International Studies University with a B.Econ. with Honors.

My research interests are in economic and financial history, household finance, and intergenerational mobility and wealth inequality.

I grew up in Hangzhou. I love writing detective novels and playing the guzheng, which I have played for sixteen years, and I hope to join a university music ensemble once I start my PhD.
